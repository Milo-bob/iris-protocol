# The Iris Protocol

Stealth-action horror in 3D, in the browser. You are Prisoner_0, an outsider who breaks into a city under the unblinking eye of a dictator. Sneak or fight your way through the detention sector, the avenues and the tower, destroy your file and bring down The Iris.

- Mouse look, WASD move, left mouse button attack, Shift sneak, Ctrl sprint, Space jump, double-tap W dash, E interact, P pause, M music.
- Built with three.js (no engine, no server needed, static files only).
- Third-party assets (CC0 Poly Haven models and textures, Mixamo characters) are listed in CREDITS.md.
